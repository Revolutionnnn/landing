FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a landing page?

Standard landing pages are delivered in 5-7 business days. More complex projects with custom integrations or multiple pages typically take 7-10 business days. We'll give you a precise timeline during the discovery phase.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. We include revision rounds to make sure the final result matches your vision. We work closely with you throughout the entire process to ensure your complete satisfaction.

Will my landing page work on mobile devices?

Absolutely. Every landing page we build follows a mobile-first approach and is thoroughly tested across iOS, Android, and all major browsers. Mobile responsiveness is a core deliverable, not an add-on.

What about SEO and page speed?

SEO and performance are built into our development process. We use semantic HTML, schema markup, optimized assets, and CDN delivery. We target Google PageSpeed scores of 90+ on both mobile and desktop.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support packages to keep your landing page updated, secure, and performing at its best. This includes content updates, performance monitoring, and continuous improvements.
